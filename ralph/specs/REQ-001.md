# REQ-001 — Electron Present F uses native setFullScreen

## Statement

On Mac Electron, Present `F` enters/exits fullscreen via main-process `BrowserWindow.setFullScreen`, not HTML `requestFullscreen`.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Preload exposes enter/exit/isFullscreen (or equivalent) on `window.poster`
- [ ] Main IPC resolves the sender’s `BrowserWindow` and calls `setFullScreen`
- [ ] Present on Electron routes `F` through the bridge (covered with present-f-uses-native)

## Primary files

- `src/adapters/electron/main.cjs`
- `src/adapters/electron/preload.cjs`
- `src/adapters/electron/presentFullscreen.cjs` (helper)
